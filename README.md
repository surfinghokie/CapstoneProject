HokieBike Beta Tester Quick Guide
INSTALL  •  SIGN IN  •  TEST
Use this guide to install HokieBike through TestFlight and sign in using the email link sent to your approved tester address.
IMPORTANT: Copy the sign-in link from the email.
THE EMAIL WILL BE IN YOUR JUNK FOLDER!
After requesting a sign-in email, copy the FULL URL from that email and paste it into the link entry bar / manual sign-in field inside HokieBike. Then tap “Complete Sign-In from Link.” Do not paste the TestFlight invite link into this field.
1  Install HokieBike with TestFlight
1.  On your iPhone, open the TestFlight invitation link shared by the HokieBike team.
2.  Tap “Start Testing.” Install the Apple TestFlight app from the App Store if prompted.
3.  In TestFlight, select HokieBike and tap “Install,” then “Open.”
2  Sign in to HokieBike — copy and paste the email link
1.  Open HokieBike and enter your approved tester email address.
2.  Tap “Continue” to request a sign-in email.
3.  Open your email inbox and find the HokieBike / Firebase sign-in message. Check Junk or Spam if needed.
4.  COPY the entire sign-in URL from the email. If tapping the link opens a browser, copy the full URL from the browser address bar instead.
5.  Return to the HokieBike app. PASTE that copied URL into the manual sign-in link bar / field on the login screen.
6.  Tap “Complete Sign-In from Link” to finish logging in.
Note: The TestFlight invitation and the email sign-in link are two different links. The first installs the app; the second authenticates your HokieBike account.
3  Updates and beta access
•	Invitations: The TestFlight invite provides access to the approved beta build; it does not sign you in.
•	Updates: When a new beta build is released, open TestFlight and tap “Update” next to HokieBike.
Troubleshooting
•	No sign-in email? Allow up to 5 minutes, check Junk/Spam, and request a new email if necessary.
•	Invalid or expired link? Request a fresh sign-in email, then copy and paste the newest full URL.
•	Link opened in Safari? Copy the complete address from Safari and paste it into the manual sign-in field in HokieBike.
•	App not in TestFlight? Confirm you accepted the correct invitation using your TestFlight Apple Account.
Need help?
Send the HokieBike team a screenshot of the error message, along with the approximate time of your sign-in attempt. Do not share your sign-in URL with other people.
